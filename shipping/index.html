Shipping policy

Bringing a puppy home should feel exciting, not confusing. We will talk through the plan with you and make sure you know what to expect at every step.

01

Let's connect

We start with a conversation about your home, lifestyle, and the puppy you have in mind.

02♡

Plan together

We discuss timing, travel options, documents, and the details before any arrangements are confirmed.

03✈

Travel with care

Where transport is available, we agree on a safe, suitable plan and share updates along the way.

04✿

Home at last

Your puppy's comfort comes first. We will help you prepare for the first days together.

✦

Please note: Travel and delivery options depend on location, age, veterinary requirements, and carrier rules. We confirm all arrangements and costs with you in writing before booking. Never send payment before verifying the seller and agreed details.
